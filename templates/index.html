Problem Statement

THE PROBLEM

In sports training, sprint performance (like a 20m dash) is usually evaluated manually by coaches. This approach:

  • → Depends heavily on human observation
  • → Lacks accuracy and consistency
  • → Cannot measure detailed parameters like stride, posture, or joint angles
  • → Makes it hard for beginners to improve efficiently
⚠️

Lack of Automated Analysis

There is a lack of an automated, accurate, and affordable system to analyze sprint performance and detect biomechanical errors such as improper posture, inefficient stride, and imbalance. This results in suboptimal performance and higher injury risk for athletes.

👁️

Manual & Unreliable Evaluation

In sports training, sprint performance (like a 20-meter race) is usually checked by coaches using their eyes. This method is not always reliable because it depends on human observation, which can be different from person to person.

🎯

Need for a Smarter System

There is a need for a system that can automatically analyze sprint performance in an accurate and affordable way. Such a system can help detect problems like wrong posture, poor stride, and body imbalance — helping athletes improve faster and reduce injury risk.

📐

Inconsistent & Incomplete Measurement

It also lacks accuracy and consistency, meaning the results may not be the same every time. Coaches cannot easily measure important details like how long each step is (stride), body posture, or angles of joints while running.

What We Measure

8 CRITICAL PERFORMANCE METRICS

Our computer vision engine extracts eight biomechanical and performance data points from every run — giving you the complete picture.

⚡

Acceleration Mechanics

Tracks how quickly you generate speed from standstill. Analyses push-off angle, forward lean, and drive phase power production across every stride.

💨

Explosive Speed

Detects your maximum velocity window and the time taken to reach it. Ideal for athletes focused on first-step quickness and breakaway bursts.

🧠

Neuromuscular Coordination

Evaluates the synchronisation between your upper and lower body. Identifies timing inefficiencies in arm swing, knee drive, and hip rotation.

⏱

Duration & Split Times

Precise timestamps for every 5M split within the 20M window, allowing comparison across attempts and training sessions over time.

📈

Velocity & Peak Speed

Calculates mean velocity across the run and identifies the exact moment and distance at which peak speed is achieved — down to the frame.

🏃

Form & Stride Efficiency

Scores your running biomechanics — head position, arm mechanics, stride length consistency, and ground contact time — against elite benchmarks.

🦵

Ground Contact Time

Measures how long each foot stays in contact with the ground per stride. Shorter contact time correlates with faster sprinting and better elastic energy return.

📊

Bilateral Symmetry Score

Compares left vs. right side mechanics to detect muscular imbalances. Asymmetry above threshold flags elevated injury risk and performance inefficiency.
